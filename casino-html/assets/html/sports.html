Sports Guide

Sports bring people together through teamwork, skill and competition. Learn the basics of several well-known sports and how to follow a match.

Popular sports

Football (Soccer)

Two teams of eleven players try to score by sending the ball into the opposing goal. A standard match consists of two 45-minute halves, with additional time when needed.

Basketball

Two teams score by shooting a ball through a raised hoop. Field goals are worth two or three points depending on distance, while free throws are worth one point.

Tennis

Players compete individually or in pairs, hitting a ball across a net. Matches are divided into games and sets, and players must keep the ball within the court boundaries.

Volleyball

Two teams separated by a net attempt to land the ball in the other team's court. Teams normally have up to three touches to return the ball.

How to follow a match

Start by checking the teams, the score, the time remaining and the competition rules. A match summary usually includes key moments, substitutions, penalties and player statistics.

Respect and fair play

Sportsmanship means following the rules, respecting officials and opponents, and supporting teammates. Practising regularly and having fun matter more than the final score.
