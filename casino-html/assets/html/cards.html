Card Games Explained

Cards have been used for centuries for memory, strategy and social games. Discover how popular card games work and what makes each one different.

Explore card games

Poker

Poker is a family of games where players compare combinations of cards. Common hands include pairs, three of a kind, straights and flushes. Reading the rules and understanding probability are key learning skills.

Blackjack

Blackjack is a comparison game in which card totals are counted toward 21. Number cards have their printed value, face cards count as ten, and an ace can count as one or eleven.

Solitaire

Solitaire is a single-player puzzle played by arranging cards into ordered sequences. Many versions challenge planning and attention, and can be played without wagers.

Bridge

Bridge is a four-player partnership game based on taking tricks. Players communicate through a structured bidding phase and cooperate with their partners during play.

Understanding a standard deck

A standard deck has 52 cards divided into four suits: hearts, diamonds, clubs and spades. Each suit contains thirteen ranks, from ace through king. Some games also include jokers.

Card games can be enjoyed as puzzles and social activities. This page provides general information only and does not include wagering or real-money games.
