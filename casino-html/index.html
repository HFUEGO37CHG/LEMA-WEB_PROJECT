Learn how reel-based games evolved, how random outcomes differ from skill-based puzzles and why probability matters. This section is informational rather than a place to play.

Our project approach

We designed this website to be easy to navigate on computers and phones. Each section has a clear heading, readable descriptions and navigation back to the other pages. We focus on learning and avoid real-money gaming or betting services.
